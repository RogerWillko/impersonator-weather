Upload the contents of site/ — index.html, compare.html, contribute.html, contribute.php, ledger.json, words.json, sources.json, robots.txt, assets/, and .htaccess — into public_html/website_734ae7cd/weather/ on Bluehost. The address is https://scrollstime.com/weather/. The Night Grade board links to that page from a brass row under the photo. That row is in the Night Grade pages, packaged as scrollstime-nav.zip in the Night Grade site folder. Do not put this page in public_html/website_df1ed662 (scrollstime.online).
