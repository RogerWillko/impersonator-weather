Where the readings sit

Impersonators

The impersonator weather is a stated range, with this station’s log still empty beside it. On 11 July 2026, Jamazel (@JRAzeltine) wrote that active impersonators are likely in the low thousands, and that dormant fakes run to the tens and hundreds of thousands. On this ruler that is Squall for the active line, from 1,000 up to Storm at 5,000, and Banked through Deep for the dormant line, from 10,000 up to Buried at 1,000,000. Followers

The follower weather is Whiteout on both dated counts. In May 2022, SparkToro and Followerwonk published 70.23% of @elonmusk’s 93,452,093 followers as unlikely to be authentic, active users who see his tweets. In August 2023, Travis Brown’s gist counted 62,500,641 follower IDs with zero tweets. Both are past 20,000 on this dial. Jamazel’s note points at those older audits, about 70% inauthentic or inactive and about 40% with zero tweets, and then states the impersonator ranges above them. A follower card answers who follows @elonmusk. An impersonator card answers who looks like him.

Active ruler: Clear 0, Breeze 1, Wind 200, Squall 1,000, Storm 5,000, Whiteout 20,000. Dormant ruler: Quiet 0, Banked 10,000, Deep 100,000, Buried 1,000,000. A range needle sits in the middle of the span the words cover. A count past Whiteout pegs at the end of the dial.

How a card got onto this page

A card needs a public page, a date, and a figure or a stated range about Elon accounts. This station’s log is the live card. Jamazel’s post is the impersonator range. The SparkToro and Followerwonk blog, and Travis Brown’s gist, are the follower counts. A live sample that changes without a pinned method date stays off the page.

The dial marks are the station’s own bands. Putting a follower count on that dial shows how large it is against the impersonator ruler. It does not turn the follower count into a count of impersonators. Frozen cards were copied on 28 September 2026. Only the station card asks the log again.
