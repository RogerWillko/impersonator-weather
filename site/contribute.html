Privacy

  • The key stays in this tab. This site and the ledger never receive it.
  • The browser sends the key only to https://api.x.com, with the words elon, musk, and maye.
  • The field is cleared when the sweep starts, and the page drops the key when the calls end.
  • What can be saved is a display name, a short note, the time, the profile count, and public handles that score 4 or higher.
  • The note is public. Words on a short block list are removed before it is saved, and again when the board is drawn.
  • Bios, follower counts, and the raw reply from X are not stored. A blank name is shown as A visitor. Each blank name stays its own row.
